Adriatic Then & Now (charts.marespot.com) pairs twenty public-domain nautical charts of the Adriatic coast — from a c. 1530 Venetian isolario to British Admiralty surveys of 1877 — with a 2003 NASA satellite view of the same sea. Every pair is a draggable before/after slider with its own deep link. All images below derive only from the licensed sources in the manifest; captions are verbatim from LICENSE-MANIFEST.json.

Boilerplate for curators

Adriatic Then & Now (charts.marespot.com) pairs twenty public-domain nautical charts of the Adriatic coast, from a c. 1530 Venetian isolario to British Admiralty surveys of 1877, with a 2003 NASA satellite photograph of the same sea. Each pair is a draggable before/after slider with a shareable deep link (for example charts.marespot.com/#chart=admiralty1877). All charts are public domain or CC0; per-image credits, source institutions and source URLs are listed in the license manifest (charts.marespot.com/LICENSE-MANIFEST.json) and under every still on this page.
